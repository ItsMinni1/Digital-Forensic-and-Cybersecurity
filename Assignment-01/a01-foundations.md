What is most dangerous for a Pakistani SME and why?
Answer: For a pakistani SME, ransomware is the most dangerous malware type since these business have minimal offsite backup and limited cybersecurity response budgets. A single attack can completely stop business operations, expose business data and threaten total financial liquidation through ransom demands. Extended operational downtime would also directly destory customer trust and business viability.
